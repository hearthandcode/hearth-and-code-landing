## Intent

## Typed contracts

- **CTP-17 Plan** — `steps: List[Step], dependencies: DAG[Step], gates: List[Gate]`. Human-readable composition: sequenced steps with dependencies and review gates. Catalog disposition: proposal.
- **CTP-21 Decision** — `choice: Choice, alternatives: List[Alternative], justification: Justification`. Human-readable composition: one choice, compared alternatives, and its reason. Catalog disposition: proposal. Use for editorial and navigation choices that remain open.
- **CTP-09 Claim** — `statement: string, evidence: List[EvidenceID], falsifier: Falsifier`. Human-readable composition: a claim tied to evidence and a condition that could disconfirm it. Catalog epistemic labels: inference or hypothesis.
- **CTP-25 Disposition** — `intent, process, knowledge, model, method, evidence, view, governance: typed`. Human-readable composition: eight parts covering intent, process, knowledge, model, method, evidence, presentation, and governance. Catalog disposition: projection.

These are descriptive source types from the Hub CTP-32 catalog. This document does not create a new interface or grant authority to publish, deploy, approve, or mark content verified.
