# Bounded Vocabulary

> 09 / Portfolio reference

The bounded vocabulary is eight terms. No ninth term is permitted without a formal amendment.

## The eight terms

1. **source** — a quoted, citable, original statement; not a paraphrase, not a summary.
2. **evidence** — an observation or measurement that supports a claim; not the claim itself.
3. **inference** — a reasoned conclusion with visible assumptions; not a source, not evidence.
4. **hypothesis** — an explanation awaiting a discriminating test; not a proposal until accepted.
5. **proposal** — a candidate artifact awaiting disposition; not a decision until sealed.
6. **projection** — a source-derived view that has been transformed; not the source.
7. **receipt** — a bounded process report; it does not authorize its own next action.
8. **unknown** — an explicit gap; named as such, not silently filled.

## Why it matters

Every claim in the working surface uses one of these eight labels. The absence of a label is itself a defect — the claim is treated as unknown. The vocabulary prevents the collapse of source into evidence, evidence into inference, or hypothesis into proposal.

## Where it lives

The bounded vocabulary is the gating vocabulary for every receipt, every review record, and every question framework instance in the Hub.
