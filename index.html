What I'm building toward

Design pillars, not a product tour. The platform is still on the drawing board — these are the bets I'm placing.

I — LEARN

Paths shaped by pedagogy

Diagnostic placement and spaced practice. Theory next to implementation, not after it. A curriculum that branches from what you actually need to learn — not a syllabus someone else already finished.

II — CRAFT

Progress that has weight

Skill trees mapped to competencies, mastery gates, artifacts you can point to. I want progress to mean you understand something — not that you cleared a checklist.

III — ASK

AI that teaches, not tells

A Socratic tutor that adapts to your goals and asks the next right question. I use AI tooling to build it; I won't let it become a chatbot that hands out finished answers.
